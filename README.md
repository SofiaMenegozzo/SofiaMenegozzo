### Hi, I'm Sofia 
Statistics Student · Data Analysis · Statistical Modeling 
I am currently pursuing a degree in Statistics and Information Management at the University of Milano-Bicocca. My academic background and interest in statistics has fostered a strong curiosity about the world of data and its potential. 
I am particularly interested in the use and analysis of data as tools for understanding, interpreting, and describing real-world phenomena.

What I like most about statistics is not just working with numbers, but asking the right questions before looking for the answers, understanding patterns, differences and relationships, and turning complex data into something meaningful and interpretable.

I work at the intersection of statistics, data analysis and real-world phenomena, from health and demographics to economics, spatial data and, more recently, human responses to AI-generated content.

I enjoy shifting between perspectives—from exploring a dataset to building a statistical model, and finally to visualizing the results and asking what they truly reveal about the phenomenon underlying the data.
